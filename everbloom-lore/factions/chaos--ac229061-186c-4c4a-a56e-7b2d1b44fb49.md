# Chaos (The Unbound)

Chaos is not a faction; it is the refusal of alignment. It is discovered by players who reject the [[The Seven Aethers|Aethers]] and [[The Light]].

## Induction
Players enter Chaos by:
1.  Refusing to choose an Aether.
2.  Speaking the phrase "[[Non Servium]]".
3.  Surviving in [[The Wilderness]] as a [[Self-Formed]] entity.

## Mechanics
* **Economy:** Chaos players can use [[Gold Economy|Gold]] but often destabilize markets, attracting [[Dragons]].
* **Measure:** Chaos players generate high, volatile [[Measure]].
* **Death:** Chaos players do not have [[Borrowed Immortality]]. If they die, their character is deleted permanently. See [[Death and Identity]].
