# The Light

The primordial force of order and possibility that existed before the world. It is not a god, but a **principle**.

## Function
* **Guidance:** The Light offers direction to players via subtle cues (glowing paths, wind, intuition).
* **The Prism:** Located in [[The First City]], it acts as the primary interface for Light-aligned players.
* **Philosophy:** The Light does not command; it invites. It seeks to maintain the experiment of creation.

## Player Alignment
Players who follow the Light:
* Receive **Orientation** (quests, waypoints).
* Do not gain raw power but gain **Trust** from systems and NPCs.
* Act as "Anchors" against [[Chaos]].
