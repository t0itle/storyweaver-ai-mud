# The Grand Luminares

A monastic order dedicated to the unwavering pursuit of truth and the eradication of falsehoods, the [[Grand Luminares]] are the foremost interpreters of [[Solastin]]'s divine will. Founded in the earliest days of [[The Crescent Kingdoms]], their austere monasteries, known as [[Lumen Spires]], are scattered throughout the realm, often built atop natural high points or significant ley lines. 

Members, often called Luminars, undergo rigorous training in logic, philosophical debate, and the discernment of subtle deceptions. While generally benevolent, their pursuit of absolute truth can sometimes lead to rigid dogma and an unyielding stance against anything perceived as 'unverifiable,' occasionally clashing with the more pragmatic approaches of other factions. They are responsible for maintaining the sanctity of the [[Oathstone of Illumination]].
