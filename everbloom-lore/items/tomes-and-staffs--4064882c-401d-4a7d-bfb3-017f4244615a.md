# Tomes and Staffs (The Amplifiers)

Since players cannot "hold" magic in their hands (they are just travelers), they need conduits.

## Staffs (The Projectors)
* **Function:** A staff is a "Barrel" for a spell.
* **Engraving:** You engrave the "Trigger" rune at the top and "Shape" runes down the shaft.
* **Example:**
    * *Top:* "Fire"
    * *Shaft:* "Distance" + "Explode"
    * *Result:* Fireball.
* **Customization:** A longer staff allows for more modifier runes (Distance, Split, Homing), but takes longer to cast.

## Tomes (The Processors)
* **Function:** A Tome is a "Circuit Board." It holds complex logic spells that run passively.
* **Engraving:** Pages are made of thin metal or treated leather that must be scribed.
* **Usage:**
    * **Page 1:** "If Hit -> Cast Shield."
    * **Page 2:** "If Low Health -> Cast Heal."
* **Limit:** Tomes are heavy and occupy the "Off-hand" slot. Opening a Tome exposes it to damage (pages can be ripped/destroyed).
