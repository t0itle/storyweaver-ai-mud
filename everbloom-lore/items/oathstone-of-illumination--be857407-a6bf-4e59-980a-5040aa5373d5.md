# Oathstone of Illumination

Within the grandest of the [[Lumen Spires]], nestled deep within [[The High Sanctum]], lies the [[Oathstone of Illumination]]. This immense, crystalline monolith is said to be a primordial shard of pure light, gifted by [[Solastin]] themselves to guide mortals towards truth. Engraved with ancient, ever-shifting symbols of clarity and law, the Oathstone hums with a faint, resonant energy. 

Those who swear oaths upon its surface are said to be bound by an unbreakable spiritual contract, as the Stone itself can discern intent. Falsehoods spoken in its presence are met with a chilling silence or, for grave deceptions, a sudden, searing flash of light that temporarily blinds the perjurer. It is a sacred artifact, revered by the [[Grand Luminares]] and occasionally sought by high-ranking officials of [[The Crescent Kingdoms]] for particularly sensitive treaties or judicial proceedings.
