# Solastin (Pride / Humility)

**The Architect.** The Aether of Civilization and Leadership.

* **Domain:** The High Sanctum (A kingdom of spires and glass).
* **Virtue (Humility):** Service to the many; building foundations.
* **Sin (Pride):** Arrogance; belief that only *you* can fix the world.
* **Followers:** Builders, Kings, Leaders.
* **Combat Style:** Defensive structures, radiant light, command buffs.
