# Lazareth (Sloth / Diligence)

**The Archivist.** The Aether of Time and Knowledge.

*   **Domain:** The [[Silent Horizon]] – A vast, crystalline desert where time itself seems to have congealed. Its mountains are petrified waves of sand, its valleys lined with the ruins of civilizations that never were, and scattered through it are libraries built from compressed starlight, holding impossible histories.
*   **Virtue (Diligence):** Deep, unwavering focus; methodical mastery of complex systems; the relentless and unbiased recording of all histories, both triumphant and forgotten. Lazareth's diligent followers seek out every shard of truth, no matter how small or hidden.
*   **Sin (Sloth):** Apathy born of overwhelming knowledge; a refusal to act due to a perceived futility in the face of inevitable entropy; the deliberate burying or obfuscation of truths that challenge established narratives, allowing silence to swallow vital information.
*   **Followers:** Scholars poring over ancient [[Tomes and Staffs]], historians meticulously chronicling every whispered rumor, and precision sniper-archetypes who believe that a single, perfectly placed shot can alter the course of written destiny. They are often robed in shades of muted grey and indigo, their movements precise and economical.
