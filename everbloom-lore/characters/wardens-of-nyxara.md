# Wardens of Nyxara

The twelve [[Wardens]] that bind [[Nyxara]] to the world. While any one of them stands, Nyxara lives (see [[Warden Destruction Rules]]).

| # | Warden | Kind | Where | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Archivist-Mirror Ish**, First Warden of Nyxara | person | Mirrorlands Court | The chief archivist whose hands open every locked memory. |
| 2 | **Gate-Mirror Tyr**, Warden of the Mirror Gate | person | Mirror Gate | A gate-warden who carries a small hand-mirror with him. |
| 3 | **Walk-Master Ven**, Warden of the Mirror Walk | person | Mirror Walk | An old master of the long mirror walk. |
| 4 | **The Thousandth Mask** | item | The Mirror Vault | A mask that has been every face its wearer envied. It waits in the vault for someone to become. Destroyed by: shatter (STR). Tended by Vault-Master Aen, Vault-Master of the Mirror Vault. |
| 5 | **Chronoglass Ot**, Warden of the Observatory | person | Chronoglass Observatory | A keeper of the great glass that records yesterday. |
| 6 | **Glassmere Warden Pel**, Warden of Glassmere | person | Glassmere | A road-warden of the lake hamlet. |
| 7 | **Reflection-Master Sar**, Warden of Reflection Rise | person | Reflection Rise | A master of the rise where the road first sees its own past. |
| 8 | **Glass-Mile Watch Tar**, Warden of the Glass Mile | person | Glass Mile | A road-warden whose lantern is a single curved sheet of glass. |
| 9 | **Veil-Mile Watch Bren**, Warden of the Veil Mile | person | Veil Mile | A road-warden in a long pale veil. |
| 10 | **The Quiet Mirror** | place | Quiet Mirror | A single mirror the size of a wall that shows who you could become, and refuses some viewers. Nyxara looks out through it. Destroyed by: desecrate (WIS). Tended by Quiet-Mirror Keeper, Keeper of the Quiet Mirror. |
| 11 | **Mirror-Pass Watch Lor**, Warden of the Mirror Pass | person | Mirror Pass | A road-warden of the high pass into the Mirrorlands. |
| 12 | **Quietshore-Sworn Inara**, Warden of Quietshore | person | Quietshore Square | A glassblower sworn to the Mirrorlands at the lake spur. |
