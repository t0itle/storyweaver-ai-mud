# Wardens of Lazareth

The twelve [[Wardens]] that bind [[Lazareth]] to the world. While any one of them stands, Lazareth lives (see [[Warden Destruction Rules]]).

| # | Warden | Kind | Where | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hush-Master Velen**, First Warden of Lazareth | person | Silent Horizon | A pale, soft-voiced master of the great hush. |
| 2 | **Gate-Hush Ari**, Warden of the Horizon Gate | person | Horizon Gate | A gate-warden who waves travelers in without speaking. |
| 3 | **Stair-Keeper Imo**, Warden of the Hush Stair | person | Hush Stair | A stair-keeper whose footfalls cannot be heard. |
| 4 | **The Glyph Hall** | place | Glyph Hall | A long hall whose walls record glyphs from every dead language. While its record holds, Lazareth remembers. Destroyed by: desecrate (WIS). Tended by Glyph-Master Ena, Glyph-Master of the Hall. |
| 5 | **The Starlight Codex** | item | Indexers Hall | A codex bound in compressed starlight that holds every history, including the ones that never were. Destroyed by: shatter (STR). Tended by Indexer Olrik, Senior Indexer. |
| 6 | **Pine-Walker Sera**, Warden of the Hush Pines | person | Hush Pines | A road-warden whose path winds between the black pines. |
| 7 | **Archive-Rider Tov**, Warden of the Archive Rise | person | Archive Rise | A messenger who carries unopened letters along the road. |
| 8 | **Ink-Watch Mae**, Warden of the Ink Mile | person | Ink Mile | A road-warden whose lantern burns black-flame ink. |
| 9 | **Hush-Watch Riv**, Warden of the Hush Mile | person | Hush Mile | A road-warden who has not spoken aloud in nine years. |
| 10 | **Hush-Master Olen**, Warden of the Hush Room | person | The Hush Room | An old master of the inner hush. |
| 11 | **Pool-Keeper Aen**, Warden of the Ink-Dark Pool | person | Ink Dark Pool | A keeper who reads the pool's surface for unspoken news. |
| 12 | **Marginalia-Sworn Fen**, Warden of Marginalia | person | Marginalia Square | A scribe sworn to the silent archive at the spur. |
