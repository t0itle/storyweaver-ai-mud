# Wardens of Velithria

The twelve [[Wardens]] that bind [[Velithria]] to the world. While any one of them stands, Velithria lives (see [[Warden Destruction Rules]]).

| # | Warden | Kind | Where | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vow-Master Ilen**, First Warden of Velithria | person | Evershade Court | The chief vow-keeper who blesses the great promises. |
| 2 | **Gate-Vow Ren**, Warden of the Vow Gate | person | Vow Gate | A gate-warden who lets only the vowed pass at night. |
| 3 | **Walk-Master Vel**, Warden of the Vow Walk | person | Vow Walk | A master of the slow vow walk. |
| 4 | **The Ribbon of First Vows** | item | Candlelit Vow Hall | The ribbon tied around the first vow ever spoken in the Evershade. It has never been untied. Destroyed by: shatter (STR). Tended by Hall-Keeper Mir, Hall-Keeper of the Vow Hall. |
| 5 | **The Tear-Glass Window** | place | Tear Glass Window | A window of set tears whose colors are wrong and beautiful. Every pure vow made beneath it strengthens Velithria. Destroyed by: desecrate (WIS). Tended by Window-Keeper Sela, Keeper of the Tear-Glass. |
| 6 | **Mourners' Tier Keeper**, Warden of the Mourners' Tier | person | Mourners Tier | A keeper of the mourners' tier. |
| 7 | **Garden-Keeper Faela**, Warden of the Veil Garden | person | Veil Garden | A garden-keeper who tends the veiled flowers. |
| 8 | **Pines-Master Onel**, Warden of the Silver Pines | person | Silver Pines | A road-warden of the pines. |
| 9 | **Mourner Rise Watch**, Warden of Mourner Rise | person | Mourner Rise | A road-warden of the mourner's rise. |
| 10 | **Silver-Mile Watch**, Warden of the Silver Mile | person | Silver Mile | A road-warden of the long silver mile. |
| 11 | **Vigil-Mile Keeper**, Warden of the Vigil Mile | person | Vigil Mile | A road-warden who keeps the night vigil. |
| 12 | **Vow-Walk Road Watch**, Warden of the Vow-Walk Road | person | Vow Walk Road | A road-warden of the long approach. |
