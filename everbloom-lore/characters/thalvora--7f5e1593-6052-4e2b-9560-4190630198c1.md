# Thalvora (Greed / Charity)

**The Merchant.** The Aether of Exchange and Value. A shimmering, ever-shifting entity draped in coin-spun silks, with eyes like polished emeralds that tally every flicker of desire.

*   **Domain:** The Gilded Bazaar – Not merely a place, but a dimension woven from countless markets, bustling souks, and silent vaults. Its air hums with bartering whispers and the chime of falling coin, where every transaction echoes through its shifting alleys and gilded domes.
*   **Virtue (Charity):** The boundless circulation of wealth and resources; the strategic investment that births [[The Crescent Kingdoms|new industries]] and fuels societal growth, ensuring no talent or innovation lies fallow.
*   **Sin (Greed):** The stagnant accumulation of capital; the exploitation of want for profit; the insidious belief that gold holds more intrinsic worth than the life it could sustain or the hands that earned it. This sin draws the ire of the [[Dragons]].
*   **Followers:** Astute Merchants, pragmatic Bankers, audacious Thieves, and all who understand that wealth is a current, whether to be channeled or diverted.
*   **Mechanic:** Followers gain significant bonuses to [[Gold Economy]] transactions, finding favorable rates, hidden opportunities, and ease in trade. However, their increasing hoards of uncommitted gold attract [[Dragons]] proportionally faster, serving as a constant reminder of Thalvora's judgmental nature toward stagnation.

## Connections
Thalvora stands as a counterpoint to [[Morvayne]]'s wild consumption, focusing on regulated exchange rather than raw intake. Her domain, the Gilded Bazaar, often touches upon the structured elegance of [[Solastin]]'s [[Lumen Spires]] for grander financial institutions, yet also brushes against the shadowed alleys where [[Nyxara]]'s followers might seek to steal secrets or transform meager beginnings into vast fortunes. The threat of [[Dragons]] links her directly to the natural world's inherent balancing forces.
