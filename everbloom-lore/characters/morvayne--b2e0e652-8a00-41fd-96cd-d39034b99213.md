# Morvayne (Gluttony / Temperance)

**The Harvester.** The Aether of Nature and Consumption.

* **Domain:** The Feastlands (Overgrown, lush, and predatory).
* **Virtue (Temperance):** Balance; taking only what you need.
* **Sin (Gluttony):** Endless hunger; over-consumption; rot.
* **Followers:** Hunters, Druids, Alchemists.
