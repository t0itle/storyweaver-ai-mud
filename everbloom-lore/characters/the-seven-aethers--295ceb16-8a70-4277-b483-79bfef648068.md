# The Seven Aethers

Elevated intelligences born from [[The Speaker]]'s resonant call, they are not gods, but the fundamental architecture of existence itself. Each Aether pulses with a unique frequency, shaping the world through its inherent nature.

## The Nature of Aethers
Each Aether embodies a profound **Virtue** and a corresponding, inescapable **Sin**. They do not rule through decree or physical force, but by a pervasive **Bias**, subtly guiding the currents of fate and the instincts of mortals (see [[Aether Intervention]]). Their influence is felt in the rustle of leaves, the ambition in a merchant's heart, or the tremor before a storm.

## Mortality
Aethers are functionally immortal, their essences woven into the fabric of reality. However, their continued existence is inextricably linked to their [[Wardens]].
* Each Aether is bound to **12 Wardens**, keystones of their spiritual presence.
* Should all 12 Wardens be destroyed, the Aether undergoes a terrifying process of **Desynchronization**, its essence fraying and tearing until eventual, inevitable oblivion.
* See [[Warden Destruction Rules]] for the grim calculus of their demise.

## List of Aethers
1.  **[[Khalvaris]]**: The Aether of Justice and Wrath. (Governance: Law, Order, Retribution)
2.  **[[Solastin]]**: The Aether of Pride and Humility. (Governance: Civilization, Structure, Enlightenment)
3.  **[[Nyxara]]**: The Aether of Envy and Kindness. (Governance: Ambition, Transformation, Creation)
4.  **[[Velithria]]**: The Aether of Lust and Chastity. (Governance: Passion, Desire, Purity)
5.  **[[Thalvora]]**: The Aether of Greed and Charity. (Governance: Commerce, Value, Exchange)
6.  **[[Lazareth]]**: The Aether of Sloth and Diligence. (Governance: Knowledge, Time, Memory)
7.  **[[Morvayne]]**: The Aether of Gluttony and Temperance. (Governance: Nature, Sustenance, Consumption)

## Relations
The Aethers, in their ancient wisdom, exhibit a deep-seated apprehension towards [[Self-Formed]] players. These individuals operate entirely outside the pre-ordained causal logic of the [[The Light]] and the Aethers' pervasive influence, representing an unpredictable variable in the carefully balanced tapestry of the world. They are anomalies, ripples in the otherwise predictable currents.

## Connections
The distinct domains of the Aethers are often reflected in the varied landscapes of [[The Crescent Kingdoms]], while their clashing 'Biases' can fuel conflicts or cultural differences between these realms. The very essence of [[The First City]]'s neutrality is a testament to its attempt to exist outside the direct influence of any single Aether.
