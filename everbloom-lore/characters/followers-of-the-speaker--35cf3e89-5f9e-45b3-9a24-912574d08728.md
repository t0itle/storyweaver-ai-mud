# Followers of the Speaker (The Veiled)

The silent priesthood that maintains [[The First City]].

## Visuals
They cover their mouths with silver cloth or masks to honor the power of **Voice**.

## Doctrine
* **Silence:** To speak for the divine is to corrupt it.
* **Neutrality:** They serve the *structure* of the world, not the [[The Seven Aethers|Aethers]].
* **Observation:** They record [[Measure]] but do not judge it.

## Role
They facilitate [[Character Creation]] at the City Gate and maintain the **Temple of Alignment**.
