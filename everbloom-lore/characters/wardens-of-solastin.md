# Wardens of Solastin

The twelve [[Wardens]] that bind [[Solastin]] to the world. While any one of them stands, Solastin lives (see [[Warden Destruction Rules]]).

| # | Warden | Kind | Where | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Theron Sun-Marshal**, First Warden of Solastin | person | High Sanctum | A grey-haired knight whose bare blade is said to keep the Beacon lit. |
| 2 | **Sister Halia**, Warden of the Pyre | person | Pyre Court | A pyre-keeper who tends the great fire night and day. |
| 3 | **The Oathstone of Illumination** | item | Oratory of Flame | A crystalline monolith said to be a primordial shard of pure light, gifted by Solastin. Oaths sworn on it bind; lies spoken before it are met with silence or a blinding flash. Destroyed by: shatter (STR). Tended by Brother Cael, Keeper of the Oathstone. |
| 4 | **The Penitents' Stair** | place | Penitents Stair | The stair the Beacon counts, step by step, each dawn. Every footfall of humility laid here holds up Solastin's light. Destroyed by: desecrate (WIS). Tended by Penitent Vren, Penitent of the Stair. |
| 5 | **Ferdan Lance-Rider**, Warden of the Causeway | person | Pilgrim Causeway | A horse-warden who escorts pilgrims along the road. |
| 6 | **Mira of the Sun-Mile**, Warden of the Sun-Mile | person | Sun Blanched Mile | A scout whose voice carries from horizon to horizon. |
| 7 | **Ash Rider**, Warden of the Pilgrim Rise | person | Pilgrim Rise | A rider whose horse has worn three sets of shoes on this rise. |
| 8 | **Kindler Ott**, Warden of the Ember Mile | person | Ember Mile | A taciturn kindler who lights the road-fires at dusk. |
| 9 | **Sun-Squire Bel**, Warden of the Foothills | person | Solar Foothills | A young squire in dented gold plate. |
| 10 | **Smith Roen**, Warden of the Solar Forge | person | Solar Forge | A smith who forges only blades meant to catch the sun. |
| 11 | **Magma-Keeper Dorn**, Warden of the Magma Forge | person | The Magma Forge | A scarred old smith who tends the deeper forge. |
| 12 | **Lantern-Keeper Selene**, Warden of the Sanctum Gate | person | Sanctum Gate | A quiet woman who keeps the great lantern at the gate. |
