# Wardens of Khalvaris

The twelve [[Wardens]] that bind [[Khalvaris]] to the world. While any one of them stands, Khalvaris lives (see [[Warden Destruction Rules]]).

| # | Warden | Kind | Where | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Verdict Tarn**, First Warden of Khalvaris | person | Iron Bastion | A black-clad judge whose ruling has never been overturned. |
| 2 | **Bailiff Korr**, Warden of the Bastion Gate | person | Bastion Gate | A heavy-shouldered bailiff who searches every cart at the gate. |
| 3 | **Forge-Magistrate Vey**, Warden of the Foundry | person | Foundry Stair | A magistrate who hears cases over the ring of hammers. |
| 4 | **Verity, the Scales** | item | Dueling Pit | The Scales of Khalvaris. Every verdict in the Bastion is weighed on them; while they balance, the Judge endures. Destroyed by: shatter (STR). Tended by Pit-Master Gren, Pit-Master. |
| 5 | **Helmsman Brod**, Warden of Helm Walk | person | Helm Walk | A retired guardsman who keeps a private watch on the wall walk. |
| 6 | **Forge-Sergeant Asra**, Warden of the Forge Rise | person | Forge Rise | A barrel-chested sergeant who drills the road-watch. |
| 7 | **Cinder-Watch Tel**, Warden of the Cinder Mile | person | Cinder Mile | A road-warden whose cloak is permanently grey with ash. |
| 8 | **Slag-Captain Yorrin**, Warden of the Slag Mile | person | Slag Mile | A captain who drills the slag-haulers like a militia. |
| 9 | **Quench-Marshal Ren**, Warden of the Slag Flats | person | Slag Flats | A marshal who keeps the cooling-yards orderly. |
| 10 | **Iron-Rider Hass**, Warden of the Ironroad | person | Ironroad | A road-rider in chain whose horse is shod in iron. |
| 11 | **Smith-Sworn Brennig**, Warden of Anvilrest | person | Anvilrest Square | A sworn smith of the bastion who runs the anvil-spur. |
| 12 | **Ardent, the Fiery Judgment** | place | The Magma Forge | The open furnace where the Bastion's judgments are tempered. The fire is a Warden: it has never gone out. Destroyed by: desecrate (WIS). Tended by Verdict Steps Korin, Judge's Second of the Magma Forge. |
