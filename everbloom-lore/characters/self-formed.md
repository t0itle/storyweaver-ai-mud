# Self-Formed

Travellers who exist entirely outside the pre-ordained causal logic of [[The Light]] and the Aethers. The Aethers fear them as unpredictable variables. Surviving in [[The Wilderness]] as one of the Self-Formed is one way into [[Chaos]].
