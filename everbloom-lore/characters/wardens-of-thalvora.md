# Wardens of Thalvora

The twelve [[Wardens]] that bind [[Thalvora]] to the world. While any one of them stands, Thalvora lives (see [[Warden Destruction Rules]]).

| # | Warden | Kind | Where | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reckoner Vex**, First Warden of Thalvora | person | Gilded Bazaar | The chief auditor of the bazaar. Her ledger has no errors. |
| 2 | **Customs-Master Lora**, Warden of the Bazaar Gate | person | Bazaar Gate | A bright, exact customs officer who never accepts a bribe. |
| 3 | **Bailiff Tov**, Warden of the Customs Walk | person | Customs Walk | A bailiff who polices the customs lane. |
| 4 | **Beggar-Captain Rin**, Warden of the Beggars' Stair | person | Beggars Stair | A beggar-king who keeps order on the lowest tier. |
| 5 | **The First Coin** | item | Coin Press | The first coin ever struck in the Bazaar, kept turning in the great press. Wealth that never stops moving. Destroyed by: shatter (STR). Tended by Pressman Coy, Pressman. |
| 6 | **Auctioneer Sel**, Warden of the Auction Tier | person | Auction Tier | A fast-talking auctioneer who runs the high tier. |
| 7 | **Stall-Boss Hena**, Warden of Black-Stall Row | person | Black Stall Row | A stall-boss who decides which traders work the black row. |
| 8 | **Debt-Marshal Olwen**, Warden of the Debt Pen | person | Debt Pen | A marshal who keeps the debtors orderly until they pay. |
| 9 | **Underbazaar Boss Trik**, Warden of the Underbazaar | person | Underbazaar | A wiry boss who keeps the under-market civil. |
| 10 | **The Vault of Flowing Coin** | place | Vault of Flowing Coin | A vault where gold is never allowed to rest. Thalvora's charity flows out of it; hoarding here would wake dragons. Destroyed by: desecrate (WIS). Tended by Vault-Keeper Fenn, Keeper of the Vault. |
| 11 | **River-Toll Mara**, Warden of the Coin River | person | Coin Choked River | A toll-keeper at the coin river. |
| 12 | **Goldwash-Master Pell**, Warden of Goldwash | person | Goldwash | A road-master at the goldwash road. |
