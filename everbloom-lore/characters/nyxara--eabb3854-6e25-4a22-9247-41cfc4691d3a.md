# Nyxara (Envy / Kindness)

**The Muse.** The Aether of Ambition and Transformation.

* **Domain:** The Mirrorlands (A shifting, illusion-filled forest).
* **Virtue (Kindness):** Empathy; helping others grow.
* **Sin (Envy):** Coveting power; sabotage; identity theft.
* **Followers:** Spies, Artists, Mages.
* **Note:** Often worshipped by Caelen (from the story) as "Bound Wisdom."
