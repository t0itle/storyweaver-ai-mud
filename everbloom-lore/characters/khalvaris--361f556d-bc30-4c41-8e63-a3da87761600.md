# Khalvaris (Wrath / Patience)

**The Judge.** The Aether of War and Law.

* **Domain:** The Iron Bastion (A volcanic, fortified land).
* **Virtue (Patience):** The discipline to wait; strategic defense.
* **Sin (Wrath):** Unchecked vengeance; scorched earth.
* **Followers:** Soldiers, Judges, Executioners.
* **Wardens:** Includes **Ardent** (The Fiery Judgment) and **Verity** (The Scales).
