# Wardens of Morvayne

The twelve [[Wardens]] that bind [[Morvayne]] to the world. While any one of them stands, Morvayne lives (see [[Warden Destruction Rules]]).

| # | Warden | Kind | Where | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Harvest-Master Brun**, First Warden of Morvayne | person | Feastlands Keep | The chief harvest-master who plans the feast. |
| 2 | **Gate-Keeper Wenna**, Warden of the Keep Gate | person | Keep Gate | A round-faced gate-keeper who hands out warm bread to children. |
| 3 | **The Seed of the First Harvest** | item | Granary Stair | A single seed from the first harvest, kept in the granary. Temperance says it must never be eaten. Destroyed by: shatter (STR). Tended by Granary-Master Olbi, Granary-Master. |
| 4 | **The Endless Table** | place | Feast Throne | The great table of the Feastlands, which is never cleared. Morvayne's bounty is laid here. Destroyed by: desecrate (WIS). Tended by Feast-Throne Watch, Steward of the Endless Table. |
| 5 | **Honey-Loft Keeper**, Warden of the Honey Loft | person | Honey Loft | A loft-keeper whose hands are permanently sticky. |
| 6 | **Furnace-Master Ged**, Warden of the Fat Furnace | person | Fat Furnace | A furnace-master who renders the great fats. |
| 7 | **Hearth-Watch Mira**, Warden of the Hearth Road | person | Hearth Road | A road-warden whose lantern burns scented oil. |
| 8 | **Grain-Mile Watch**, Warden of the Grain Mile | person | Grain Mile | A road-warden of the long grain road. |
| 9 | **Orchard-Mile Watch**, Warden of the Orchard Mile | person | Orchard Mile | A road-warden between the orchards. |
| 10 | **Harvest-Rise Watch**, Warden of the Harvest Rise | person | Harvest Rise | A road-warden of the rise. |
| 11 | **Barley-Flats Watch**, Warden of the Barley Flats | person | Barley Flats | A road-warden of the flats. |
| 12 | **Cider-Hollow-Sworn Pel**, Warden of Cider Hollow | person | Cider Hollow Square | A sworn smallholder of the keep at the orchard spur. |
