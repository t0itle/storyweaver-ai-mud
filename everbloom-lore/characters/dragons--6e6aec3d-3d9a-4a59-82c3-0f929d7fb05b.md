# Dragons

Ancient, autonomous entities from [[The Wilderness]] that act as the world's **Economic Regulators**.

## The Hoard Mechanic
Dragons are attracted to **stagnant value**.
* If players or cities hoard uncommitted [[Gold Economy|Gold]], a Dragon is spawned.
* **Behavior:** They attack vaults, disrupt trade routes, and consume wealth.

## Function
Dragons prevent inflation and hoarding. They are forces of nature, not malice.
* **Killing a Dragon:** Releases massive amounts of Gold back into the economy (creating inflation/chaos).
* **Surviving a Dragon:** Requires spending Gold (defense, tribute), thus solving the hoarding problem.
