# Wardens

The keystones that bind each of [[The Seven Aethers]] to the world. Every Aether is bound to **twelve Wardens**. A Warden may be a **person**, an **item** or a **place**.

## The Rule
**An Aether lives while any one of its Wardens still stands.** When the last falls, the Aether undergoes **Desynchronization** and falls. See [[Warden Destruction Rules]].

## Kinds
* **People:** sworn guardians (gate-wardens, masters, road-wardens). A slain Warden does not return.
* **Items:** relics that rest in a sacred room, such as the [[Oathstone of Illumination]].
* **Places:** sites that are themselves sacred to the Aether, such as Ardent, the Fiery Judgment of [[Khalvaris]].
