# Solastin

One of [[The Seven Aethers]], Solastin embodies **radiant truth and unyielding justice**. Born from the primordial light that pierced the initial void, some say directly from a refraction of [[The Speaker]]'s initial creative impulse, Solastin is the eternal sentinel against deception, corruption, and the subtle creeping darkness of [[Chaos]]. Their essence is clarity, their will, unwavering law. Solastin is also associated with **Pride** and **Humility**, specifically the arrogance that obscures truth versus the humble dedication to its revelation.

## Domain
The purview of Solastin extends beyond simple judgment; it encompasses the *revelation* of truth. They are called upon in oaths, sought for guidance in clouded times, and revered as the ultimate arbiter of cosmic balance. Where mysteries persist and shadows gather, Solastin's light is believed to eventually find purchase, illuminating all that is hidden. Their domain, often referred to as [[The High Sanctum]], is a [[Crescent Kingdoms]] realm of towering spires and reflective surfaces, meticulously designed to capture and amplify natural light, mirroring Solastin's own illuminating nature.

## Appearance
Solastin appears as an incandescent figure, often described as a living sunbeam or a form woven from pure, golden light, without discernible flesh or bone. Their presence is said to bring warmth and pierce shadows, causing those with ill intent to recoil. Unlike the more martial [[Khalvaris]] or enigmatic [[Lazareth]], Solastin's visage is one of pristine, unblemished light.

## The Beacon and the Blade
Solastin's power manifests in two primary ways:
*   **The Beacon:** A pervasive, spiritual light that seeks out falsehoods and offers clarity. It is the inspiration behind many ancient laws and codes of conduct, often found within the doctrines of orders like the [[Followers of the Speaker]]. This aspect resonates with *Humility*, as it guides rather than dictates.
*   **The Blade:** A cutting truth that can excise corruption and bring swift, decisive justice. This aspect is less about mercy and more about the surgical removal of blight, often represented by devastating, purifying blasts of energy that can rival the destructive force of a [[Dragons|dragon's]] fury when roused. This is where *Pride* in one's own righteousness can manifest, leading to uncompromising judgment.

## Influence
Cultures dedicated to Solastin often prioritize order, transparency, and logical deduction. Their architecture might feature towering spires and reflective surfaces, designed to capture and amplify natural light. They are often staunch opponents of [[Chaos]], seeing it as the ultimate obfuscation of truth and a direct affront to the structured reality [[The Speaker]] articulated. Many paladins and inquisitors across the lands are said to draw their conviction from Solastin's radiant will, often wielding amplified truths through [[Tomes and Staffs]] inscribed with sacred geometry.

## Connections
*   [[The Speaker]]: Solastin's light is said to originate from [[The Speaker]]'s initial creative act, connecting Solastin to the fundamental source of all reality.
*   [[The Seven Aethers]]: As one of the Aethers, Solastin represents a core virtue (Humility) and sin (Pride) that shapes a fundamental aspect of the world, specifically truth and justice.
*   [[Chaos]]: Solastin stands in direct opposition to Chaos, embodying clarity and order against the Unbound's rejection of structure.
*   [[The High Sanctum]]: This is Solastin's direct domain, a physical manifestation of their principles of light and structure within [[The Crescent Kingdoms]].
*   [[Followers of the Speaker]]: The emphasis on truth and ancient laws in Solastin's domain aligns with the doctrines of the veiled priesthood in [[The First City]].
*   [[Khalvaris]]: While both Aethers deal with justice, Solastin focuses on revealing truth, while Khalvaris (Wrath/Patience) governs the execution of justice through warfare and law, making them complementary but distinct forces.
