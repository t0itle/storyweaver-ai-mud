# Aether Intervention

The Aethers do not rule by force but by **bias**. In their own kingdoms that bias is close to rule: the people of a kingdom live under its Aether.

In turn, each living Aether wakes, looks over its kingdom and issues decrees to its people: goals for particular subjects, laws for a season, rumours, festivals, bonds between people. Its proclamations are heard at its throne and capital.

Otherworlders are free. An Aether can only **nudge** them: summons and invitations sent to those sworn to it, never commands.
