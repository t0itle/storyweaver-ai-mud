# Non Serviam

*"I will not serve."* The phrase that forsakes the [[The Seven Aethers|Aethers]] and binds the speaker to [[Chaos]]. Spoken at **the Hidden Fissure** of [[The First City]].

It is irreversible, and the whole world hears it: a ripple of events and rumours spreads from the place it is spoken. Those who speak it then walk Chaos's own seven-step path, which ends in hunting an Aether's [[Wardens]].
