# The Shadow

The whisper of [[Chaos]]. For every word the [[The Speaker|Speaker]] spoke, an unsaid thing was pushed aside; the Shadow is what was not chosen, given a hunger. It collects in the unspoken places: the open dunes, wells gone dry, rooms where an old promise was broken.

Travellers who refuse every Aether, or linger in [[The Wilderness]], hear it offering to finish their sentences. Listening deepens their corruption; resisting pushes it back. Those who give in may speak [[Non Serviam]].
