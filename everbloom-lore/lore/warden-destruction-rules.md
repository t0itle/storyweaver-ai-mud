# Warden Destruction Rules

The grim calculus of an Aether's death.

1. Each Aether is bound to twelve [[Wardens]]: people, items and places.
2. **An Aether lives while any one of its Wardens still stands.**
3. **People** are destroyed by being slain. A slain Warden never returns.
4. **Items** (relics) are destroyed by **shattering** them where they rest: a test of Strength against the relic.
5. **Places** are destroyed by **desecrating** them: a ritual test of Wisdom while standing within.
6. A failed attempt draws the Warden's backlash and wounds the attacker. A Warden can be tested only once an hour by the same hand.
7. Those sworn to an Aether cannot raise a hand against its Wardens.
8. When the last Warden falls, the Aether **desynchronizes**: it falls, its kingdom drifts without its bias, and no one it once sheltered is carried home at death.
