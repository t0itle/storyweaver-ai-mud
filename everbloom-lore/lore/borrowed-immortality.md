# Borrowed Immortality

The sworn do not truly die while their Aether lives. When a traveller sworn to a living Aether falls, that Aether carries them to its city well, alive at half strength.

* If their Aether has **fallen**, it can only carry them home stripped of coin and gear.
* Those with **no Aether** (and all of [[Chaos]]) have no borrowed immortality. See [[Death and Identity]].
