# Character Creation

Happens at **the Gate of Form** of [[The First City]], facilitated by the [[Followers of the Speaker|Veiled]].

A newcomer chooses:
* a **name** and a short description of themselves (the Veiled see them as they are: a humble traveller in plain clothes);
* a **race**: see [[Races]];
* a **class**: see [[Classes]].

Their body is shaped by both: hit points and six **ability scores** (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma) start at 10, raised or lowered by race and class. Every uncertain deed in the world is a dice roll plus these scores.

Next, they choose their [[Alignment]].
