# Death and Identity

* **Downed:** a traveller beaten in a fight lies downed and must win a death save.
* **Sworn:** see [[Borrowed Immortality]].
* **Unsworn:** death is death. A body may be raised by another for a price (50 gold), but it returns as a zombie.
* **[[Chaos]]:** canon says a Chaos traveller who dies is deleted permanently: there is no one to carry them home. *(Not yet enforced by the game; for now Chaos follows the unsworn rule.)*
