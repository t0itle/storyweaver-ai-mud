# Prehistory

The generated history of the world before player arrival (approx. 300 years).

## The Epochs
1.  **The First Speaking:** Creation of the City and Kingdoms.
2.  **The Crescent Wars:** Early conflicts that solidified borders.
3.  **The Age of Stabilization:** Rise of Guilds, Banks, and the Veiled.
4.  **The Quiet Rot:** Current era. Stagnation, hoarding, and the return of [[Dragons]].

## State at Launch
* All 7 [[The Seven Aethers|Aethers]] are alive.
* [[The Crescent Kingdoms]] are stable but brittle.
* Lore is contradictory (different myths in different lands).
