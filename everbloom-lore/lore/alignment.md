# Alignment

In **the Hall of Seven Ways** in [[The First City]], every traveller chooses.

* **Swear to an Aether.** Kneel and bind yourself to one of [[The Seven Aethers]]. One-time. The sworn walk a **seven-step vow chain** for their Aether, are carried home by it at death while it lives (see [[Borrowed Immortality]]), and may receive its nudges (an Aether rules its NPCs, but cannot command a player).
* **Walk out.** Refusing to choose is remembered: each refusal adds chaos pressure, and the Shadow's whispers grow (see [[The Shadow]]).
* **[[Non Serviam]].** Forsake all seven and join [[Chaos]]. Irreversible.

Followers of [[The Light]] receive orientation and trust rather than power.
