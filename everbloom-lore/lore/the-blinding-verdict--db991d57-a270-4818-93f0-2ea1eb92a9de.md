# The Blinding Verdict

Referred to in hushed tones among the [[Grand Luminares]], [[The Blinding Verdict]] is a legendary event, a powerful manifestation of [[Solastin]]'s cutting truth. Lore speaks of a singular moment in [[Prehistory]] when a cabal of powerful sorcerers allied with [[Chaos]] attempted to permanently shroud a vast portion of [[The Crescent Kingdoms]] in illusions and falsehoods. 

In response, [[Solastin]] is said to have unleashed a devastating, purifying blast of light—a 'Blade' made manifest—that not only shattered the illusions but also permanently stripped the cabal of their sight and their ability to lie, making them unwitting heralds of truth. The land scarred by this event, now known as the 'Scarred Peaks,' is still considered cursed by those with deceitful hearts, and it serves as a stark reminder of the Aether's unyielding justice. This devastating power is often compared to the destructive force of a [[Dragons|Dragon's]] fury, yet its purpose is purification rather than destruction.
