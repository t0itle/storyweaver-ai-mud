# Otherworlders

The world's name for travellers (the players): people who wake on the Crescent Shore, or wander in from [[The Wilderness]], unable to recall how they came. The [[Followers of the Speaker|Veiled]] welcome them into [[The First City]] and see them through [[Character Creation]] at the Gate of Form.

Because they are free to choose, the Aethers cannot command them, only court and nudge them. Some swear to an Aether; some refuse; some become [[Self-Formed]].
