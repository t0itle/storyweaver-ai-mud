# Races

The peoples an Otherworlder may be born as at the Gate of Form (see [[Character Creation]]). Each race shifts the six ability scores from a base of 10.

## Human
Adaptable wanderers from the old kingdoms. Unremarkable, and therefore everywhere.

* **Gift:** Balanced — gain extra starting gold and a second guess in any conversation.
* **Cost:** No standout edge in body or sense.
* **Abilities:** WIS +1, CHA +1; HP +0.

## Elf
Slender folk of the long memory. They listen for things others cannot hear.

* **Gift:** Keen perception — see lore hints others miss; +1 to discovery checks.
* **Cost:** Frail frame. -3 max HP.
* **Abilities:** DEX +2, WIS +1, CON -1; HP -3.

## Dwarf
Stone-shouldered hill folk who measure trust in decades.

* **Gift:** Hardy. +4 max HP and resistance to fear effects.
* **Cost:** Slow — initiative penalty in combat. Distrustful of magic.
* **Abilities:** CON +2, STR +1, CHA -1; HP +4.

## Half-Giant
Children of the Whispering Wastes, where the old titans still sleep.

* **Gift:** Massive. +8 max HP; can shoulder doors and intimidate weaker foes.
* **Cost:** Conspicuous — cannot enter low buildings; merchants charge double.
* **Abilities:** STR +3, CON +1, DEX -1, CHA -1; HP +8.

## Beastman
Pact-born of the deep wilds — wolf, bear, falcon, or feline kin.

* **Gift:** Senses of the wild — track scents, hear whispers; +1 to all examine actions.
* **Cost:** Distrusted in cities. Cultists may refuse you passage.
* **Abilities:** WIS +2, DEX +1, CHA -1; HP +0.

## Sandborn
Desert-blooded nomads who can hear the dunes turn over in their sleep.

* **Gift:** Heat immunity; double water reserves; the snake of the oasis remembers your face.
* **Cost:** Cannot stand cold. -2 HP in any northern region.
* **Abilities:** CON +1, DEX +1, WIS +1, INT -1; HP +0.
