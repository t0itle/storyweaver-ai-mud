# Gold Economy

Gold is meant to move. [[The First City]] is the safest place to store it before it is committed to the world: banks there and in the towns hold coin and goods.

* **Markets** set live prices; towns levy tariffs.
* **Hoarding wakes [[Dragons]]:** great stagnant hoards draw them to vaults and trade roads.
* [[Thalvora]]'s followers trade on better terms, and attract Dragons faster.
* NPCs earn wages, pay for meals and company, and pay the rewards on the jobs they post.
