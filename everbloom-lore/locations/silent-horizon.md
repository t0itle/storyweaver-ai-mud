# Silent Horizon

[[Lazareth]]'s kingdom in [[The Crescent Kingdoms]]: a vast crystalline desert where time itself seems to have congealed. Its mountains are petrified waves of sand, its valleys lined with ruins of civilizations that never were, and its libraries are built from compressed starlight and carved into canyons whose entrances are guarded by petrified scholars.

* **Ruler:** [[Lazareth]], the Archivist.
* **Seat:** the Archive Throne.
* **Notable:** the Glyph Hall, whose walls record every dead language; the Indexers' Hall; the Hush Stair.
* **Wardens:** see [[Wardens of Lazareth]].
