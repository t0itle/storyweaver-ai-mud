# The First City

The neutral convergence point of the world. It acts as an interface between the [[The Wilderness]] and [[The Crescent Kingdoms]].

## Governance
It is maintained, not ruled, by the [[Followers of the Speaker]] (The Veiled). No [[The Seven Aethers|Aether]] holds dominion here, ensuring free choice.

## Key Locations
* **The Gate of Form:** Where [[Character Creation]] occurs for those seeking recognition.
* **The Hall of Seven Ways:** The chamber where players choose their [[Alignment]].
* **The Hidden Fissure:** A secret location where the phrase "[[Non Serviam]]" can be spoken to discover [[Chaos]].

## Economy
The city is the primary ledger for [[Measure]]. It is also the safest place to store [[Gold Economy|Gold]] before it is committed to the world.
