# Lumen Spires

These towering, often crystal-infused structures serve as both monasteries and bastions of the [[Grand Luminares]]. Each [[Lumen Spires|Spire]] is meticulously designed to amplify natural light, with complex arrays of polished lenses and reflective surfaces that channel solar and lunar rays into their central meditation chambers, known as the 'Truth-Glow Core.' It is here that Luminars believe the purest essence of [[Solastin]]'s radiance can be accessed for contemplation and guidance. 

Many of the [[Lumen Spires]] contain ancient libraries of illuminated texts and are built around unique geological formations rumored to possess inherent clarifying properties, such as the [[Oathstone of Illumination]] found within the highest Spire of [[The High Sanctum]]. They are focal points for those seeking honesty and clarity within [[The Crescent Kingdoms]].
