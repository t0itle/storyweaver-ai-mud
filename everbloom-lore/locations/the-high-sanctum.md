# The High Sanctum

[[Solastin]]'s kingdom in [[The Crescent Kingdoms]]: crystalline spires that touch the clouds, rivers flowing like liquid light through impossibly symmetrical cities. Its towers are built to capture and amplify natural light.

* **Ruler:** [[Solastin]], the Architect.
* **Seat:** the Beacon Throne, above the Sanctum.
* **Notable:** the [[Lumen Spires]] of the [[The Grand Luminares|Grand Luminares]]; the [[Oathstone of Illumination]].
* **Wardens:** see [[Wardens of Solastin]].
