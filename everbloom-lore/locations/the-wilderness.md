# The Wilderness

The untamed, unpredictable land beyond [[The Crescent Kingdoms]], and the constant pressure against their order. [[Dragons]] come from here. Those who survive here outside any alignment may become [[Self-Formed]].

In the game: the roads, wilds, deserts, groves and shores between towns, where random encounters, camping ambushes and the deep-desert wander happen.
